THE ART OF SCENT

Maison Élixir. Experience the pinnacle of luxury fragrances, hand-blended and aged to perfection in Lagos.

Our Story

Maison Élixir was born from a singular vision: to craft luxury fragrances that capture the bold, dynamic essence of Lagos while utilizing the highest standards of international high-end perfumery.

Every bottle is a testament to our slow maturation philosophy. We don't rush. We don't mass-produce. We source raw, organic materials—precious oud wood, rare damask roses, and sweet vanilla—and let them steep in organic oils over months.

THE MATURATION PROCESS

01. Sourcing

We source pure essential oils and extracts, partnering with ethical organic growers to gather raw oud, rare rose petals, and citrus essences.

02. Aging

Our perfumers age each fragrance blend for a minimum of six months. This slow maturation allows the top, middle, and base notes to seamlessly harmonize.

03. Bottling

Each bottle is filled, sealed, and inspected by hand, before being nestled in our signature high-end packaging.

CONSCIOUS LUXURY

We believe luxury shouldn't cost the Earth. By sourcing ingredients from sustainable local farms and using hand-blown recyclable glass bottles, we craft premium fragrances that respect both nature and community.
