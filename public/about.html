THE HOUSE OF MAISON ÉLIXIR

Born in Lagos.
Matured in Silence.

We craft luxury fragrances for discerning connoisseurs. Blending the world's rarest botanical extracts with an unyielding commitment to artisanal maceration.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

The Craft of Uncompromising Quality

Maison Élixir was founded on a simple principle: true luxury cannot be mass-produced. In an era dominated by synthetic acceleration, our atelier chooses the deliberate path of slow maturation.

Every bottle originates from raw essential oils handpicked across Grasse, Madagascar, Calabria, and Morocco. Our master perfumers blend each formula by hand, steeping them in darkness for six full months until the top, heart, and base notes merge into an indelible signature scent.

OUR PILLARS

The Foundation of Our Atelier

01

Rare Botanical Extracts

We source pure, unadulterated extracts—wild-harvested oud, Calabrian bergamot, and hand-picked Damask rose petals directly from ethical growers worldwide.

02

Six-Month Maturation

Time is our most valuable ingredient. Each batch macerates for half a year in temperature-controlled darkness to unlock maximum sillage and longevity.

03

Artisanal Precision

From oil extractions to hand-filling and wax sealing, every detail is executed with meticulous craftsmanship in our Lagos atelier.

THE JOURNEY

From Botanical Sourcing to Bottle

01. Ethical Sourcing

Partnering directly with small-scale, sustainable farms in Grasse and Africa to obtain rare essential oils with pristine purity.

02. Six-Month Aging

Allowing complex fragrance compounds to bond organically over 180 days, cultivating deep, multi-layered scent evolutions on skin.

03. Atelier Bottling

Hand-poured into heavy crystal glassware, polished, cataloged, and sealed with our signature emblem before release.
